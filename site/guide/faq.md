## Is my data readable by anyone else?

No. Backups are encrypted on your Mac before anything is uploaded, and the
repository password is stored only in your macOS Keychain.

## Am I locked into Keelhaven?

No. Backups are standard restic repositories — you can list, verify, and
restore them with the open-source restic CLI on any machine, even without
Keelhaven installed.

## Is Keelhaven open source?

The app itself is a paid, closed-source product. It builds on the open-source
restic backup engine, and your backup data always remains accessible with
open-source tools.
